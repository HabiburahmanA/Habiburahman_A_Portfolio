# 🧠 My Business Analysis Approach

I approach business and product problems using an eight-step framework:

### 01 — Understand

Understand the business problem, user need and expected outcome.

### 02 — Analyse

Identify workflow gaps, dependencies, pain points and root causes.

### 03 — Define

Convert business needs into clear requirements and acceptance criteria.

### 04 — Document

Create structured requirements, workflows, SOPs and supporting documentation.

### 05 — Coordinate

Work with stakeholders and cross-functional teams to move requirements toward implementation.

### 06 — Validate

Use UAT, testing and stakeholder feedback to confirm that the expected outcome has been achieved.

### 07 — Improve

Identify inefficiencies and opportunities for process or product improvement.

### 08 — Measure

Evaluate the outcome using appropriate business, operational or product metrics.
